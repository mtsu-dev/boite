# Settings

Settings holds everything you can change in Boite, on nine pages. Each page
answers one question. The other pages of this guide name a setting as
Settings, then the page, then the card, such as Settings, Input, Voice.

## Open Settings

- Press Ctrl+, or the gear in the app rail. Settings opens on Appearance.
- Search settings, at the top of the list of pages, finds a page, a card or a
  single setting by its name, its explanation or a related word. Enter opens
  the first result and highlights its card.
- Back to threads returns to the conversations.

## The nine pages

| Page | The question it answers |
| --- | --- |
| Appearance | What Boite looks like. |
| Input | How you talk to Boite: the keyboard and your voice. |
| Conversations | How threads are listed and how agents answer. |
| AI accounts | Which AIs you use, and how much is left. |
| Agents | What agents know and how they run. |
| Safeguards | What agents are allowed to do. |
| Devices | Which devices connect to which machines. |
| Version | Stable or ahead. |
| Troubleshooting | What is wrong, and what to send when you ask for help. |

The "i" beside a page's title says the same.

## Where a setting applies

The line under a page's title says where most of its cards apply:

- This device: the computer, phone or browser you are using.
- This machine, with its name: the machine that runs the agents. With several
  machines connected, pick another one there.
- All your machines: every machine of your group.

A card that applies to all your machines, on a page that does not, says so
beside its title.

## Who sees what

- Agents, and some cards of other pages, belong to the owner. A phone or a
  browser added as a device does not show them.
- The Experiments card on Version exists in Nightly builds only
  ([experiments](experiments.md)).
- On a phone, Settings lists the same pages, those the phone may open.
